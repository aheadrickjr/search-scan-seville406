How to play

Fill the 6×6 grid so that every row, every column, and every 2×3 outlined box contains the numbers 1 through 6 exactly once.

Tap a cell, then tap a number to fill it in. Use Notes to pencil in candidates, Hint to reveal the correct value for the selected cell, and Undo/Erase to fix mistakes.
